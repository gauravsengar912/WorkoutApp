4-Day Upper / Lower Split Workout Plan
Optimized Home Program Integrating Pull-Up Bar, Barbells, Dumbbells & Resistance Tubes
🧱 Pull-Up Bar 🏋️ Straight Barbell 🏋️ EZ-Curl Bar 💪 Dumbbells 🎯 Resistance Tubes
Weekly Schedule & Architecture:

A 4-day Upper/Lower split allows for higher training frequency, greater volume per muscle group, and better recovery than a 3-day split.

Recommended Split: Mon: Upper A | Tue: Lower A | Wed: Rest | Thu: Upper B | Fri: Lower B | Sat/Sun: Rest / Active Recovery.

Progression & Recovery Rules

  • Pull-Up Progression: If bodyweight pull-ups are difficult, loop your resistance tube over the bar and place a foot/knee in it for assistance. If you can perform >12 easy reps, hold a light dumbbell between your feet.
  • Progressive Overload: Focus on increasing weight plates or rep counts every 1–2 weeks while maintaining strict form.
  • Rest Periods: Rest 90–120 seconds for main compound exercises (Pull-Ups, Squats, Presses, RDLs) and 60 seconds for isolation moves.
